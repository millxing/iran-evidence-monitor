07 / METHODOLOGY

Measure. Challenge. Revise.

A scale, not a probability

−5 very strong challenge; −4 strong challenge; −3 moderate challenge; −2 limited challenge; −1 slight challenge; 0 inconclusive; +1 slight support; +2 limited support; +3 moderate support; +4 strong support; +5 very strong support. Δ is the current level minus the provisional November 12 baseline. Equal numeric steps do not imply equal amounts of evidence. Missing data never count as negative evidence.

Attribution has limits

These six hypotheses come from the supplied analytical report, pages 6–9. They include interpretive extensions of Pape’s work. They are not verified direct quotations. No composite score or assessment of Pape as a person is produced.

A controlled update process

Scheduled checks detect changes to registered source pages. They do not decide causation. A reviewer must approve test definitions, then record evidence IDs, rationale, uncertainty and a signed assessment. Related observations share a cluster to prevent double counting.

A historical cutoff

The baseline cutoff is November 12, 2026, 23:59:59 UTC. Sources must have been released by that date. Retrieval after the cutoff cannot prove the content was unchanged. Baseline levels are provisional analyst judgments until archived versions and the source set are reviewed. Future observations must not enter the baseline.

The initial release implements the report’s evidence-monitoring MVP. Daily AIS, insurance, treaty, economic and military feeds are not connected. No backtested probability model is claimed.
